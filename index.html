Before you begin

Are the calculations free?

Yes. The core tools in this version require no paid API or account.

How accurate is the Jathagam?

The astronomy layer is designed to calculate planetary positions from birth time and location. For professional-grade ephemeris work, exact historical timezone/DST handling and divisional charts, use a validated Swiss Ephemeris service/backend.

Is astrology scientifically proven?

No. Astrology is a traditional belief/practice. The site should be used for cultural, personal and entertainment purposes rather than as a substitute for professional decisions.
